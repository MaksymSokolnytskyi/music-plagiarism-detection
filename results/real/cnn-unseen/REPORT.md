## Exploratory: local matching

`chroma_local` slides a 30 s window over the original and finds its best subsequence-DTW match anywhere in the suspect, under all 12 transpositions. It was designed *after* the full-track table showed global alignment failing, and is evaluated on the same pairs, so treat it as a hypothesis check, not a result.
